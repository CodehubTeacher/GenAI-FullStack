MODERN HISTORY

The Renaissance:
- Revival of classical learning in Europe.
- Sparked humanism, art, and scientific discovery.

The Enlightenment:
- Age of Reason (17th–18th centuries).
- Thinkers like Voltaire, Locke, and Rousseau emphasized liberty and reason.

Industrial Revolution:
- Began in Britain in the 18th century.
- Introduced steam engines, mechanized factories, and railways.
- Shift from agrarian to industrial societies.

World Wars:
- WWI (1914–1918): Triggered by assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand.
- WWII (1939–1945): Nazi aggression, global conflict, atomic bombs.

Cold War:
- U.S. vs. Soviet Union (1947–1991).
- Space Race, nuclear arms race, ideological clash between capitalism and communism.
