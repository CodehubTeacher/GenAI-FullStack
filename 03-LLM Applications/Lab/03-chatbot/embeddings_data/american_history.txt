AMERICAN HISTORY

Native American Civilizations:
- Advanced societies like the Maya, Aztec, and Inca.
- Rich traditions, agriculture, and architecture.

Colonial America:
- 1607: Jamestown, first permanent English settlement.
- 1776: Declaration of Independence, American Revolution against Britain.

Civil War (1861–1865):
- Conflict between North (Union) and South (Confederacy).
- Main issue: slavery.
- Led to abolition of slavery.

20th Century:
- U.S. emerged as a world power.
- WWII involvement (1941–1945) shaped global politics.
- Civil Rights Movement (1950s–1960s) fought racial segregation.

Modern Era:
- Space exploration: 1969, first moon landing.
- Tech revolution: Silicon Valley, internet boom.
