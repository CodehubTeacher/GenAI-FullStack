ANCIENT HISTORY

Mesopotamia:
- First civilization (c. 3500 BCE).
- Developed writing (cuneiform), wheel, irrigation.
- Code of Hammurabi – one of the first legal systems.

Ancient Egypt:
- Civilization along the Nile River.
- Pharaohs considered divine rulers.
- Built pyramids as tombs; advances in mathematics and medicine.

Indus Valley Civilization:
- Flourished c. 2500–1900 BCE in present-day India and Pakistan.
- Planned cities like Mohenjo-Daro and Harappa.
- Advanced drainage and trade systems.

Ancient China:
- Shang and Zhou dynasties.
- Invention of bronze casting, early writing, and philosophies like Confucianism.
