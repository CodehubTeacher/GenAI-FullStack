HISTORICAL FIGURES

Alexander the Great (356–323 BCE):
- Conquered much of the known world by age 30.
- Spread Greek culture across Asia.

Cleopatra (69–30 BCE):
- Last Pharaoh of Egypt.
- Known for alliances with Julius Caesar and Mark Antony.

Mahatma Gandhi (1869–1948):
- Leader of Indian independence movement.
- Advocated non-violent resistance.

Abraham Lincoln (1809–1865):
- 16th U.S. President.
- Ended slavery with the Emancipation Proclamation.
- Assassinated in 1865.

Albert Einstein (1879–1955):
- Developed theory of relativity.
- Revolutionized modern physics.
