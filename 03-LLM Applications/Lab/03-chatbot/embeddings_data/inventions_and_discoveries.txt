INVENTIONS AND DISCOVERIES

Printing Press (1450, Gutenberg):
- Revolutionized knowledge sharing.
- Mass production of books.

Steam Engine (18th century):
- Fueled the Industrial Revolution.
- Enabled trains, factories, and modern transport.

Electricity (19th century):
- Harnessed by scientists like Faraday and Edison.
- Led to light bulbs, telegraphs, and power grids.

Internet (20th century):
- Initially a U.S. defense project (ARPANET).
- Now connects billions worldwide.
