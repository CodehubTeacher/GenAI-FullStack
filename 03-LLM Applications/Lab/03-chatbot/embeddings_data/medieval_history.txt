MEDIEVAL HISTORY

Feudalism:
- Dominant political system in Europe (9th–15th century).
- Kings granted land to nobles in exchange for loyalty and military service.

The Crusades:
- Religious wars (11th–13th centuries) between Christians and Muslims.
- Main goal: control of Jerusalem and the Holy Land.
- Increased trade and cultural exchange between Europe and the Middle East.

Mongol Empire:
- Founded by Genghis Khan in 1206.
- Became the largest contiguous empire in history.
- Encouraged trade across Silk Road, religious tolerance, and cultural diffusion.

European Renaissance (14th–17th century):
- Rebirth of art, science, and learning.
- Key figures: Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, Galileo.
