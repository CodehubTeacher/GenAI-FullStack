WORLD WARS

World War I (1914–1918):
- Trigger: Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand.
- Major powers: Allies vs. Central Powers.
- Trench warfare, poison gas, machine guns.
- Treaty of Versailles ended the war, imposed harsh terms on Germany.

World War II (1939–1945):
- Trigger: Nazi invasion of Poland.
- Major powers: Allies (U.S., U.K., USSR) vs. Axis (Germany, Italy, Japan).
- Key events: Pearl Harbor, D-Day, Holocaust, Hiroshima & Nagasaki.
- Outcome: Defeat of Axis powers, formation of United Nations.
