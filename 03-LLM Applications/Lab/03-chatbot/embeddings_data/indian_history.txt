INDIAN HISTORY

Indus Valley Civilization:
- Earliest known Indian civilization (c. 2500 BCE).
- Urban planning, trade, and writing system.

Maurya Empire (321–185 BCE):
- Founded by Chandragupta Maurya.
- Ashoka spread Buddhism across Asia.

Gupta Empire (320–550 CE):
- Golden Age of India.
- Advances in mathematics (concept of zero), astronomy, and literature.

Mughal Empire (1526–1857):
- Founded by Babur.
- Akbar’s reign marked religious tolerance and cultural growth.
- Taj Mahal built during Shah Jahan’s rule.

British Raj (1858–1947):
- Colonization led to economic changes and independence movement.
- Mahatma Gandhi led non-violent struggle.
- India gained independence in 1947, partitioned into India and Pakistan.
